Helping Hands Charity

About Us

Helping Hands is dedicated to providing shelter, food, and educational programs to homeless communities. Our goal is to empower individuals to rebuild their lives and contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To eradicate homelessness through compassionate support, community engagement, and sustainable resources that foster growth and independence.
